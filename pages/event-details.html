AI & Machine Learning Workshop

Explore the exciting world of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning in this hands-on workshop designed for college students.

About the Event

This workshop introduces students to the fundamentals of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and their real-world applications. Participants will learn about machine learning concepts, data preparation, model building and practical applications.

Whether you are a beginner or already interested in AI, this workshop will help you understand how modern intelligent systems are built.

What You'll Learn

  • Introduction to Artificial Intelligence
  • Machine Learning fundamentals
  • Working with real-world datasets
  • Building your first ML model
  • Industry applications of AI
